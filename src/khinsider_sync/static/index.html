    Formats and download order
      Tick the formats you want and move them up or down. Files download in this order: everything in the top format first (across all albums), then the next. FLAC is often 3 to 5 times the size of MP3. An album with none of the ticked formats isn't downloaded.
      A ticked platform brings in these album types
      None ticked = every type. Albums you tick one by one are always synced.
      Go easy on khinsider
      khinsider publishes no limit; its Cloudflare protection starts asking for a browser check when a connection reads pages too fast. When a limit is reached the sync stops and carries on by itself as soon as there is room again - a big list spreads over hours or days.
